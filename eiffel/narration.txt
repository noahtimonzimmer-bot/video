## HOOK
A man sold the Eiffel Tower. Twice.
## STORY
See, Victor is a smooth-talking con man in nineteen twenty-five Paris, and one morning he reads in the newspaper that the tower is rusting, and repairs cost a fortune.
So Victor prints fake government letters and invites six scrap metal dealers to a secret meeting in the finest hotel in town.
He tells them the city wants to sell the tower for scrap. Seven thousand tons of iron.
But it must stay secret, or the people will riot.
One dealer, André, wants it so badly that he pays Victor the money, plus a bribe on top.
That same night, Victor is on a train out of Paris, with a suitcase full of cash.
André is so embarrassed that he never tells the police.
So a few weeks later, Victor comes back… and sells the tower again.
This time the buyer goes to the police, and Victor barely escapes.
## END
!And the Eiffel Tower? It was never for sale. It's still standing today.
