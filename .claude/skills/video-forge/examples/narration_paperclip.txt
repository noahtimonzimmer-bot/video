## HOOK
This guy traded a single paper clip… for an entire house.
!And no, this is not a joke.
## STORY
See, Kyle MacDonald was sitting at his computer in two thousand five, with a red paper clip on his desk.
And then he had a crazy idea.
What if he kept trading that paper clip… for something just a little bit better?
So he put it online.
And two women actually traded him a pen shaped like a fish.
Kyle traded the pen for a doorknob with a smiley face.
The doorknob became a camping stove.
The stove became a generator.
And then the trades got bigger and bigger.
A snowmobile. A trip into the mountains. Even a delivery truck.
His story spread across the internet…
and suddenly, everyone wanted to be part of it.
A recording contract. A year of free rent. An afternoon with a rock star.
And after fourteen trades, the unbelievable happened.
A small town in Canada offered him a real house.
In return, the town got a role in a real movie… and held auditions for it.
## END
Kyle never bought that house.
!He traded his way there… one paper clip at a time.
