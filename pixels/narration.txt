## HOOK
This twenty-one-year-old became a millionaire with just one website…
!and he didn't even sell a single product.
## STORY
See, Alex Tew was a broke student in England who needed money for university.
So he came up with an idea that sounded completely ridiculous.
He built a website with exactly one million pixels…
and started selling them. One pixel, one dollar.
Businesses could buy a spot and put their ad on it.
At first, almost nobody cared.
But then the newspapers picked up the story…
and suddenly, everyone wanted a piece of his website.
Pixels were selling like crazy.
When only the last thousand were left, Alex put them up for auction…
and they sold for thirty-eight thousand dollars.
In just a few months, his website made over one million dollars.
## END
And the craziest part? The website is still online today.
He didn't invent anything new.
!He just figured out how to turn attention into money.
