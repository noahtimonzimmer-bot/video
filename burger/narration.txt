## HOOK
This guy took a hundred and fifty thousand dollars from a fast food giant…
!and it was completely legal.
## STORY
See, Chris is a broke computer science student who wants to study in Japan.
But he's broke as hell. Forty-two dollars in his bank account.
One day, a burger contest caught his attention. Collect the codes on your cups, and you could win a million dollars.
But Chris didn't buy a single burger.
Instead, he walked up to the cleaners behind every restaurant in town… and offered to take their trash. For free.
So night after night, he dragged home thousands of dirty cups.
And he typed every single code into a program he wrote himself.
For days, nothing happened.
But then, at two a.m., his screen turned green.
## REVEAL
Because the codes weren't random.
They followed a formula… based on the print date and the factory number.
So Chris could predict exactly which boxes, on which days, carried the big winners.
He drove to those restaurants… and bought just one cup each time.
And two weeks later, he had a hundred and fifty thousand dollars.
!He didn't break a single rule. He just read the system… and booked his flight to Tokyo.
