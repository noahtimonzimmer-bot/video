## HOOK
This guy took a hundred and fifty thousand dollars from a fast food giant…
!and it was completely legal.
## STORY
Meet Chris. A broke computer science student.
His dream: one semester in Japan. His bank account: forty-two dollars.
Then a burger chain launched a contest. Collect the codes on your cups… win up to a million dollars.
Chris didn't buy a single burger.
Instead, he went to the cleaners behind every restaurant in town…
and offered to take their trash. For free.
Night after night, he dragged home thousands of dirty cups.
He typed every single code into a program he wrote himself.
Days passed. Nothing.
Then, at two a.m., his screen turned green.
## REVEAL
The codes weren't random.
They followed a formula… based on the print date and the factory number.
Chris could now predict which boxes, on which days, carried the big winners.
He drove to those restaurants… and bought one single cup each time.
Two weeks later: a hundred and fifty thousand dollars.
!No rules broken. He just read the system… and booked his flight to Tokyo.
