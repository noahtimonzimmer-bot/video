## THE HOOK
You're standing on the roof of a forty-story building.
Your girlfriend is hanging from the edge.
You're holding her hand.
And your shadow is holding a knife.
But here's the terrifying part.
Your shadow isn't pointing the knife at her.
It's pointing it at YOU.
You scream.
And your shadow raises its arm.
!THREE DAYS EARLIER.
## DAY ONE
You wake up late for work.
You brush your teeth.
Put on your shoes.
And walk outside.
Everything seems normal.
Until you notice something strange.
Your shadow is waving.
But you're not.
You stop walking.
It stops too.
You raise your right hand.
Your shadow raises its left.
You laugh.
Maybe you're just tired.
Then you turn around.
And your shadow stays where it is.
You stare at it.
It stares back.
Well…
It doesn't have eyes.
But somehow, you know it's watching you.
You take one step backward.
Your shadow takes one step forward.
You run.
It follows.
But not the way a shadow should.
It runs beside you.
## THE FIRST WARNING
You rush into your apartment.
Lock the door.
And turn off every light.
Your shadow disappears.
You finally relax.
Then your phone lights up.
And your shadow appears on the wall.
Except now…
It's knocking.
Three times.
You turn off your phone.
Silence.
Then you hear three knocks on your front door.
You look through the peephole.
Nobody's there.
But something has been scratched into the wood.
!DON'T GO TO WORK.
You think someone's playing a prank.
So you ignore it.
Twenty minutes later, you arrive at your office.
You step into the elevator.
Your shadow stays outside.
It grabs the elevator doors.
And refuses to let them close.
You pull your foot back.
The doors shut.
The elevator rises.
Then…
A deafening crash.
The elevator falls.
Straight to the basement.
You would have been inside.
!Your shadow just saved your life.
## IT KNOWS THE FUTURE
That night, you decide to test it.
You put a lamp on the floor.
Your shadow appears on the wall.
You ask:
"Can you understand me?"
It nods.
You freeze.
"Can you see the future?"
It nods again.
You grab a piece of paper.
"Write something."
Your shadow points toward the kitchen.
You follow it.
On the refrigerator is a photograph of you and your girlfriend, Emma.
Your shadow points at Emma.
Then slowly draws a line across its own throat.
You stumble backward.
"No."
Your shadow points at the clock.
Eleven fifty-nine P.M.
You call Emma.
No answer.
You call again.
Still nothing.
Then you receive a message from her.
"Someone is standing outside my window."
You grab your keys.
And run.
## THE OTHER SHADOW
You reach Emma's apartment.
Her door is open.
The lights are flickering.
"Emma?"
No answer.
You enter the bedroom.
And find her hiding beneath the bed.
She's shaking.
You help her up.
"What happened?"
She points toward the wall.
There's a shadow standing there.
A human-shaped shadow.
But nobody is casting it.
Your own shadow moves in front of you.
Like it's protecting you.
The other shadow tilts its head.
Then smiles.
Not with a mouth.
With a thin white crack across its face.
Your shadow grabs your arm.
And pulls you toward the door.
You run.
Behind you, the other shadow begins crawling across the ceiling.
You reach the stairs.
And suddenly every light in the building goes out.
The creature disappears.
But so does your shadow.
And for the first time…
!You realize you need it.
## THE RULES
You and Emma escape into the street.
Under the streetlights, your shadow returns.
It looks exhausted.
Almost smaller.
You ask it what that thing was.
Your shadow points at the ground.
Then at Emma.
Then at you.
You don't understand.
So Emma takes out her phone.
She opens an old photograph.
It's a picture of you from five years ago.
You're standing beside her.
Smiling.
But there's something wrong.
Your shadow is facing the camera.
Even though you're facing away.
You zoom in.
Your shadow is holding something.
A small black key.
Emma whispers:
"I remember that day."
You look at her.
"What happened?"
She swallows.
"You were in an accident."
You laugh nervously.
"What accident?"
Her eyes fill with tears.
!"You died."
## YOU WERE NEVER SUPPOSED TO WAKE UP
Five years ago, you were hit by a car.
Your heart stopped for four minutes.
Doctors brought you back.
But Emma tells you something she never mentioned before.
When you woke up, you kept saying the same sentence.
"Something followed me back."
You don't remember any of it.
Your shadow suddenly begins moving.
It points at your chest.
Then at the ground.
Then it lies perfectly still.
You understand.
It isn't predicting your death.
It's remembering it.
You ask:
"Are you the thing that followed me?"
Your shadow shakes its head.
Then points behind Emma.
You turn.
Under the streetlight, Emma has two shadows.
One moves with her.
The other doesn't.
The second shadow slowly stands up.
Emma screams.
And both of you run.
## THE CITY OF SHADOWS
You race through the streets.
But now you notice them everywhere.
A man waiting for a bus has three shadows.
A woman walking her dog has none.
A child standing beneath a streetlight has a shadow twice his size.
Something is happening to the entire city.
Your shadow pulls you toward an abandoned building.
Inside is a staircase.
You climb.
Floor after floor.
Until you reach the roof.
Your shadow points toward the horizon.
The sun is setting.
And every shadow in the city is getting longer.
Emma asks:
"Why did it bring us here?"
Then you notice the black key.
It's lying on the roof.
Exactly like the one in the old photograph.
Beside it is a locked metal door.
You open it.
Inside is a single mirror.
And written above it are five words:
!ONLY ONE OF YOU BELONGS HERE.
## THE TRUTH
You look into the mirror.
You see yourself.
Emma.
And your shadow.
But in the reflection, your shadow is standing upright.
Like a real person.
It steps forward.
And speaks.
"You were never supposed to survive that accident."
You stumble backward.
Your shadow continues.
"When your heart stopped, something tried to take your place."
You look at Emma.
Her second shadow is crawling up the wall.
Your shadow points at it.
"That thing has been waiting five years."
You whisper:
"Why?"
"Because it cannot enter the living world without a body."
Emma grabs your hand.
Then her second shadow suddenly pulls her backward.
Straight toward the edge of the roof.
You catch her wrist.
She slips over the side.
And now…
You're standing on the roof of a forty-story building.
Your girlfriend is hanging from the edge.
You're holding her hand.
And your shadow is holding a knife.
## BACK TO THE HOOK
Your shadow raises the knife.
You scream:
"STOP!"
But it doesn't stop.
It brings the blade down.
Straight toward your hand.
You close your eyes.
And hear a sharp crack.
You open them.
Your shadow hasn't stabbed you.
It has cut through the black shape wrapped around your wrist.
The second shadow.
It was using Emma to pull you over the edge.
The creature screams.
Your shadow grabs it.
And drags it toward the mirror.
The mirror begins to crack.
You pull Emma onto the roof.
The two shadows disappear into the glass.
Then the mirror shatters.
Silence.
You look down.
Your shadow is gone.
For the first time in your life…
!You don't cast one.
## THE FINAL TWIST
Three weeks pass.
You and Emma move to a new apartment.
Everything feels normal again.
One morning, you wake up.
Sunlight fills the bedroom.
You look at the floor.
Your shadow is back.
You smile.
"You're alive."
It waves.
You wave back.
Then Emma walks into the room.
She kisses you.
And leaves for work.
You watch her go.
Your shadow slowly turns toward you.
Then points at the bedroom mirror.
You look.
In the reflection, Emma is still standing behind you.
Smiling.
Even though she just left.
You turn around.
Nobody's there.
Then the reflection whispers:
!"You saved the wrong one."
