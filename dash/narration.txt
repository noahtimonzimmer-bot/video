## HOOK
In my city, nobody walks. Ever.
!Huss. Huss. Huss.
## STORY
See, my city is called Dash. And the only rule is: if your feet touch the ground, you run.
To school. Huss, huss, huss.
To the bakery. Huss, huss, huss.
Grandma runs to the mailbox. The dog runs to the dog.
Weddings are relay races. Birthday cakes get caught mid-air.
!Nobody ever asked why. We just… ran.
But one day, a new kid moved here.
He got off the bus, looked at all of us sprinting past him…
!and he walked.
!The whole street stopped. Silence.
And that's when we heard it. Under our feet. A rumble.
!Because the ground in Dash isn't ground at all.
## REVEAL
It's the back of something huge.
!And the only thing keeping it asleep… is the sound of our feet.
!So, new kid… huss, huss, huss.
