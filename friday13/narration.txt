## HOOK
Why is Friday the thirteenth unlucky?
It might be because of what one king did… on that exact day.
## STORY
France, thirteen-oh-seven.
King Philip the Fourth is broke.
And he owes a fortune to the most powerful order in Europe.
The Knights Templar.
Warrior monks. Bankers to kings. Untouchable.
So Philip makes a plan.
He sends sealed orders to every corner of France.
Orders that must not be opened… until one exact morning.
Friday. October thirteenth.
At dawn, the seals break.
And all across the kingdom, at the same moment… the soldiers strike.
Hundreds of Templars are dragged from their beds.
Tortured. Forced to confess to crimes they never committed.
Seven years later, their Grand Master, Jacques de Molay, is burned alive in Paris.
And legend says, from the flames… he cursed the king.
Within the year, King Philip was dead.
!Coincidence? Or the reason we still fear Friday the thirteenth?
