## HOOK
She asked for three birds from every house…
!and burned an entire city with them.
## STORY
Kiev, nine forty-five.
A tribe called the Drevlians murders Prince Igor.
Then they make a fatal mistake.
They send men to marry his widow… Olga.
She greets them with honor.
And buries them alive… in their own boat.
She asks for their noblest men next.
They are led to a bathhouse. The doors are locked.
Then… the fire.
At her husband's funeral feast, the Drevlians drink…
until her soldiers draw their swords.
Then she besieges their city, Iskorosten.
For a whole year… it will not fall.
So Olga makes an offer.
"I want no more revenge. Only a small gift."
"Three pigeons and three sparrows… from every house."
The city laughs. And pays.
That night, her men tie burning sulfur to every bird…
and set them free.
The birds fly home. Back to their nests. Under the straw roofs.
Every house catches fire at once.
!The woman who did this… was later declared a saint.
