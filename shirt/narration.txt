## HOOK
This guy made over a million dollars… just by wearing T-shirts.
## STORY
See, back in two thousand eight, Jason Sadler had a weird idea.
What if companies paid him to wear their shirts?
So he built a website and put every single day of the next year up for sale.
January first cost one dollar. January second, two dollars.
And December thirty-first? Three hundred sixty-five.
Whoever bought a day got Jason. He wore their shirt, took the photos, shot the videos, and posted everything online.
At first, it was just an experiment.
But the idea spread… and more and more companies wanted in.
His first year sold out completely.
So he hired more people to wear paid shirts too.
In the end, his company made over a million dollars.
## END
Jason figured out that anyone can be a billboard.
He sold the one thing he did every day anyway… getting dressed.
!And a few years later? He sold his last name too.
