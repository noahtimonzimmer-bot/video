## HOOK
In my town, everybody has to play the same game. Every single day.
!Hus. Hus. Hus.
## STORY
See, the rules are simple. You wrap the burger. Three folds.
!Left… hus. Right… hus. Up… hus!
Perfect timing gives you points. And you need points.
Because every night at midnight, the monster comes.
!And whoever has the fewest points… is just gone.
So everybody plays. My teacher plays under the desk.
My grandma plays at the bus stop. My dog plays with his nose.
Nobody sleeps. Nobody talks. You only hear it from every window.
!Hus. Hus. Hus.
Last night, my best friend had three points less than me.
!In the morning, his house was empty. Only his cap was lying on the doorstep.
So I opened the game to play for both of us.
!And I froze.
The burger on my screen… was wearing his cap.
!Every burger we've ever wrapped… was someone who lost.
I wrapped him as carefully as I could. Hus. Hus. Hus.
!Perfect.
!And somewhere in town, a door creaked open.
I didn't sleep. I wanted to know who the monster was.
So I checked the high score list.
Number one. For forty-seven years. The same name.
!My grandma.
At midnight, the big shadow walked down our street.
!And under it, I saw two fluffy pink slippers. Grandma's slippers.
She stopped in front of my window. Smiled. And tapped on the glass.
!"Sweetie… tonight, the lowest score in town… is yours."
!My phone says twelve seconds to midnight.
I need points. And I can't do it alone.
So if you're watching this… play. Every point you wrap counts for me.
!Hus. Hus. Hus.
!Part two… if I'm still here.
